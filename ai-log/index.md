# FHLBible — ai-log index

FHLBible is the full-featured Bible reader iOS app (multiple translations, full-text search, Strong's numbers / parsing, commentary, audio Bible, offline SQLite). It is one of three sibling projects in the workspace: FHLBible, FHLBibleTW (Taiwanese-Hokkien edition), FHLCommon (shared framework). Minimum iOS 17.0 (was 14.0, then 15.0; see the 2026-10-21 logs).
